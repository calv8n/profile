About

BIT Mesra · CSE

I'm an AI engineer at PwC in Hyderabad. For two years I've built LLM agents that people use at work: an HR assistant in Teams for 1,500+ employees, and a questionnaire agent that turned a 3–4 day job into about two hours.

What I care about most is whether you can trust the output. FinSight runs code instead of guessing numbers. My document-extraction project is judged on calibration, not just accuracy. Right now I'm working on how to evaluate the questionnaire system when there's no labeled production data, which is harder than it sounds.

Before PwC I studied CSE at BIT Mesra, did a research internship on music generation at Illinois Institute of Technology and published a GAN paper at ICMIA 2023.
